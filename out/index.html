The LIFT Method

FOUR PILLARS.
ENDLESS PROGRESS.

Built on science-backed programming designed specifically for women who want to lift heavy and get stronger every week.

01
Strength Programming

Evidence-based periodization built for women. Progressive overload across 8-week cycles with deload weeks built in — no guesswork.

02
Technique Mastery

Every class starts with a movement clinic. We film your lifts, review form on screen, and refine mechanics before loading the bar.

03
Community Power

Training alongside women who share your goals changes everything. Our floor culture is competitive, supportive, and unapologetically loud.

04

Fueling for strength looks different than fueling for cardio. Our coaches help you eat to perform, recover, and hit new PRs every cycle.
